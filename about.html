Who we are

100% Irish owned. Farm to fork.

Gaelic Burger is Irish owned from top to bottom. We keep things farm to fork: good ingredients, cooked to order and served hot.

You'll find us in Newhall, just outside Naas, with outdoor seating and a proper welcome. Our breakfast bap is served all day, 7 days a week, alongside burgers, cheese steak and chips cut fresh to order from Irish grown potatoes.
