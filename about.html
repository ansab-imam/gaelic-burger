Gaelic Burger is Irish owned from top to bottom. We believe a great burger starts with great Irish produce, so we keep things farm to fork: good ingredients, cooked to order and served without shortcuts.

Our chips are cut fresh, our portions are generous, and every burger is built the way we'd want it ourselves.
