Privacy Policy

Short and simple.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What we collect

This website does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There are no forms, no accounts and no tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads the fonts on this site. Google may receive your IP address when fonts load.
  • Google Maps is embedded on our Find Us page and may set its own cookies when it loads.
  • Online ordering takes place on Deliveroo, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Social links take you to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Tripadvisor, each with their own policies.

Hosting

This site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps basic server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and performance.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. As this site collects none, there is usually nothing to request, but you can contact us on +353 45 916 090 with any questions.
