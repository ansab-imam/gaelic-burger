Allergen info

Got an allergy or intolerance? Talk to us before you order and we'll help you choose.

Ask us: +353 45 916 090
Best inLeinster2021

Our food is prepared in a kitchen that may handle any of the 14 major allergens. We take care to avoid cross-contamination, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from allergens.

Please ask our team in person, or call +353 45 916 090, before you order. If you order online, check the allergen details shown with each item on Just Eat.

The 14 allergens

  • Cereals containing gluten
  • Crustaceans
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Peanuts
  • Soybeans
  • Milk
  • Tree nuts
  • Celery
  • Mustard
  • Sesame
  • Sulphites
  • Lupin
  • Molluscs

Recipes and suppliers can change, so please check with us every time you order.
