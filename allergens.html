Eat safe

Allergen Info

Got an allergy or intolerance? Talk to us before you order and we will look after you.

Our food is prepared in a kitchen that handles all 14 major allergens. While we take care to prevent cross-contamination, we cannot guarantee any dish is completely free from allergens.

Full allergen details for every item are available from our team. Please ask in person, or call +353 45 916 090 before you order. If you order online, check the allergen details shown with each item.

The 14 allergens

  • Cereals containing gluten
  • Crustaceans
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Peanuts
  • Soybeans
  • Milk
  • Nuts
  • Celery
  • Mustard
  • Sesame
  • Sulphites
  • Lupin
  • Molluscs

Recipes and suppliers can change, so please check with us every time you order.
